# FOUR WARNED: REGENT

## World Building & Style Guide

v0.1

---

## 1. Setting Overview

Four Warned: Regent takes place in a grounded medieval frontier world inspired by:

- medieval England
- Irish frontier settlements
- Norse influence
- isolated river communities
- seasonal survival

The setting emphasises:

- realism
- practical governance
- environmental hardship
- regional identity
- political fragility

---

## 2. Tone & Atmosphere

### Core Tone

The world should feel:

- grounded
- weathered
- practical
- politically tense
- atmospheric
- melancholic
- resilient

NOT:

- heroic fantasy
- high magic
- theatrical medievalism
- exaggerated fantasy tropes

---

## 3. Visual Identity

### 3.1 Practical Medieval Realism

Architecture:

- timber halls
- palisades
- stone keeps
- muddy roads
- functional villages

Clothing:

- rough wool
- leather armor
- cloaks
- practical tools
- weather-worn fabrics

---

### 3.2 Frontier Survival

Settlements should feel:

- isolated
- vulnerable
- resource-dependent
- hard-earned

The environment is beautiful but dangerous.

---

### 3.3 Natural Atmosphere

Key environmental elements:

- fog
- rainfall
- torchlight
- muddy roads
- snow-covered mountains
- dense forests
- wheat fields
- rivers

Weather and seasons should strongly influence mood.

---

## 4. Geography

### Core Regions

Riverlands

Trade and agriculture.

Southern Forests

Dense woodland, bandits, timber.

Western Mountains

Stone, iron, harsh winters.

Northern Farmlands

Food production and open countryside.

## 5. Political Structure

Governance is:

- decentralised
- fragile
- personality-driven

The kingdom relies on:

- governors
- stewards
- reeves
- merchants
- marshals

Communication is slow.

Local authority matters.

---

## 6. Institutional Aesthetic

The UI and writing should evoke:

- royal ledgers
- council correspondence
- wax seals
- illuminated manuscripts
- scribal annotations

The interface should feel:

> governed, not gamified.

---

Writing should feel:

- concise
- practical

- intelligent
- restrained

Avoid:

- modern slang
- excessive fantasy terminology
- melodrama

---

### Governance Terminology

Preferred terms:

- decree
- charter
- mandate
- levy
- grain reserve
- frontier watch
- steward
- reeve
- marshal
- council

- tax burden

Each advisor should:

- sound distinct
- reflect regional priorities
- reveal personality subtly

Example:

Steward

Measured, cautious, practical.

Marshal

Direct, urgent, militaristic.

Merchant

Transactional, opportunistic.

Scribe

Calm, intelligent, analytical.

## 9. UI / UX Style

### Interface Philosophy

The game should feel like:

- reading reports
- signing decrees
- reviewing ledgers
- receiving correspondence

NOT:

- operating dashboards
- spreadsheet software
- sci-fi terminals

---

### UI Materials

Preferred:

- parchment textures
- dark wood
- iron
- candlelight
- faded ink
- worn paper

Avoid:

- neon
- bright fantasy colors
- excessive ornamentation

### Regent's Authority Interface System

The production UI should follow the "Regent's Authority" style system:

- Dark parchment and iron surfaces.
- Candlelight gold as the primary interactive accent.
- Faded crimson for negative or martial actions.
- Forest green for prosperity and success.
- EB Garamond for headings, Literata for body copy.
- A centered, fixed-width 1120px ledger layout.
- Subtle rounded corners only; avoid pill-shaped controls.
- No glassmorphism or neon treatment.
- Buttons should feel like wax seals.
- Inputs should feel like quill lines.
- Cards should feel like parchment decrees.
- Lists should read as ledgers with ruled horizontal separators.

## 11. Thematic Core

The world is fundamentally about:

- survival through cooperation
- fragile institutions
- governance under uncertainty
- the burden of leadership
- scaling complexity

The kingdom is never fully stable.

That tension defines the setting.
